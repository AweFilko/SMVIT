Light Potion

A keychain flask that glows when your phone gets a notification. It flickers for a moment, then fades away.

Drag the flask to turn it, tap the button on the cork to switch it on or off, and press the notification button to see it glow.

Standby
Interactive 3D model of the current design

A note on the design. The potion flask is the first design, not the final one. The electronics do not depend on the shape, so later versions can look different: other bottles, charms or small objects.

How it works

1

Android app

Listens for notifications and reads only the category.

2

Bluetooth LE

Sends two bytes to the flask: category and urgency. Never the message text.

3

Flask brain

An ESP32-C3 picks a colour: call green, message blue, email yellow.

4

Glow

Three LEDs flicker for 2–3 seconds, then fade out.

The full process

Design

Cork lid button

A latching push button on top switches the battery fully on or off.

USB-C on the base

Charge it from the bottom of the flask.

Frosted glass body

The whole flask glows, not just a single dot.

Brass collar and key ring

A small loop on the neck keeps the lid free for the button.

Inside

≈ 8 days

per charge, an estimate that still has to be measured

XIAO ESP32-C3

Battery charger and 3.3 V regulator on the board.

250 mAh LiPo

Protected cell, small enough for a flask about 35 mm wide.

3 WS2812B LEDs

Switched off by a MOSFET when idle to save power.

  • GPIO3 high turns Q1 on, which pulls Q2’s gate low and powers the LEDs. Low or floating keeps them off.
  • The firmware sets GPIO4 low before the LED power is switched off.
  • The board charges the battery from USB-C only while the lid button is on.
